Is Orbi a real coach?

Orbi is an AI coach. It reads your logged training and goals, and every number it suggests comes from Ora's tested training rules, which are built on published research. It explains its reasoning and never changes your plan without your OK.

Do I need Ora Pro?

No. Logging, progress, rewards and friends are free. Pro adds Orbi's coaching, smart programs and weekly check-ins.

What about my data?

Your training lives on your phone first. You can export it or delete it at any time, and Orbi only sees what it needs to coach you.
