Ora / Progress tracker
Trend review

Progress tracking that explains the signal window.

Ora brings training, nutrition consistency, body metrics, and check-ins into one review surface while calling out stale or missing data.

What Ora supports

  • Recent workouts, sets, exercises, and weekly training volume.
  • Nutrition consistency beside body metrics and check-ins.
  • Upload and appearance check-in recency without making photos the product.
  • Warnings when a trend window is too sparse to support confident conclusions.

Why it matters

A useful progress tracker helps review trends without overreacting to one session, one partial food day, or one body-weight entry.

Progress tracker FAQ

How does this connect to reports?

Ora keeps captured data, source quality, and missing context together so review packets can show both signal and uncertainty.

Does Ora replace coaching judgment?

No. Ora organizes evidence and feedback boundaries. Athletes and coaches still decide what to do with the review.

Ora is developed by Eric Zhu for lifters who want training, nutrition, progress reports, and coach review in one accountable system.
