Accessibility

Ora for everyone.

Our accessibility goal for oracoach.app, where we know it still falls short, and how to tell us about a problem.

Effective November 15, 2026

Our Goal

We want oracoach.app to work for everyone, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, zoom or larger text. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 (the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) at level AA.

What we do today:

  • A Skip to Content link at the top of each page, for keyboard and screen reader users
  • Visible focus outlines on links, buttons and form fields
  • Text descriptions for meaningful images, and labels on form fields
  • Pages that fit phone screens and still work when you zoom in
  • If your device asks for reduced motion, the homepage turns off its drifting backgrounds and entrance animations, Orbi stays still, and sections jump into place instead of gliding

Known Limits

We're still working on these:

  • The 3D Orbi on the homepage is animated and moves between sections as you scroll. You can only spin him by dragging or tapping, not with a keyboard. He's decoration, and nothing on the page depends on him.
  • Reduced motion isn't everywhere yet. We respect it where we've added it, but a few small effects still move, such as buttons lifting on hover and the pulsing "Training now" dot.
  • Section scrolling on large screens: on a desktop, the homepage moves one section at a time when you scroll or use the arrow keys.
  • App screenshots on the homepage are images. Each has a short description, but not every word on the screen.

Tell Us About a Problem

If something on oracoach.app is hard to use, or you need information in another format, email support@oracoach.app. Tell us the page and what got in the way. A real person reads every email. Problems in the Ora app are welcome too.
