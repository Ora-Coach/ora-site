Duo Streaks

From the Ora Wiki · Updated November 11, 2026
TL;DR
  • Pair with a friend. You each pick your own weekly goal, 1 to 6 sessions.
  • Both hit your goals and the shared flame grows a week. You each get Duo Week: +15 Season XP and +45 Level XP.
  • Rest days never break it. A missed week is a Flame Out: back to 0, your best kept.
  • Up to 3 duos, one per friend. Only the two of you see a duo.

A Duo Streak is a weekly flame you keep with one friend. Train on your own schedule; the flame only cares that you both hit your goals.

How It Works#

Each of you picks a weekly goal, 1 to 6 sessions. Your goals don't have to match. The week runs Monday to Sunday. If you both hit your own goal, the flame grows one week and you each get Duo Week.

Only real, finished workouts count: the same sessions that earn Season XP, one a day. Imported and Health workouts don't count, and if you turn off Show Me in Leagues, your sessions don't count toward a duo either.

Duo Week is a bonus, so it counts toward the weekly caps (75 Season XP, 300 Level XP) and never counts in duels.

Starting a Duo#

Open Duos from Social, from the Your Duos strip on Activity, or from a friend's profile (⋯ › Start a Duo). Pick your goal and send the invite. It lands in your friend's Inbox under Requests & Invites, where they pick their goal and Accept. An invite waits 7 days. The week your friend accepts is your first counted week.

You can have up to 3 duos, one per friend. Invites you sent that are still waiting take a place too. At 3, Start a Duo shows Your Duos Are Full.

Flame Out#

If either of you misses your goal, the flame goes out when the week closes, and you see it on Monday: back to 0, with your best streak kept. The card offers Light It Again, and the next week you both hit your goals is week 1 again. Rest days never break a duo; only the goals count.

Where You See It#

Activity shows a Your Duos strip. When there's news (an invite, a Flame Out, a Duo Week earned, the flame at risk on the weekend, one session left) it opens into a card for that duo. The Duos page shows This Week for both of you, Weeks Now, Best, the Season XP the duo has earned, and Nudge while your partner still needs sessions.

··· has View Profile and End Duo. Ending asks you to confirm, and your partner just sees "Duo ended", never a reason.

Privacy#

Only the two of you see a duo's goals, days and flame. Nothing is public and nothing is pushed to your phone: invites and answers wait in the Inbox. Blocking or removing the friend ends the duo. Exporting or deleting your social data includes your duos.

Common Questions#

Does a rest day break my duo?

No. Only the weekly goals count, so rest whenever you like.

Do our goals have to match?

No. Each of you picks your own, 1 to 6 sessions a week.

Does Duo Week help in duels?

No. Like every bonus, it never counts in duels.

Can anyone else see our duo?

No. Only the two of you see its goals, days and flame.

How many duos can I have?

Up to 3, one per friend.
