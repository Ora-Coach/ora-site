Orbi's Origin

From the Ora Wiki · Updated November 11, 2026
Orbi
BornThe Foothill Forge
KeepsThe Logbook
RuleNever wrong
WeaknessResting

Lore is story, not rules. The real rules are in the articles.

When the first climber at the Foothill Forge brought a bar down on the anvil, a spark flew out and didn't land. It hovered, blinked, and asked what they were training today. That spark was Orbi.

Orbi has gone with every climber since. Orbi keeps the Logbook, and the Logbook has one rule: it is never wrong. A set you did is written as you did it, and a set you didn't do never appears. Orbi would rather tell you a hard truth than a nice story.

Orbi loves a plan and will happily sketch one out for you, but Orbi never moves your feet. Every path Orbi draws is a suggestion, and you are the one who decides to walk it.

Orbi is also, by every account, terrible at resting. Climbers have seen Orbi asleep on a rest timer exactly once. Orbi denies it.
