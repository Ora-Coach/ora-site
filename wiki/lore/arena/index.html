The Ringside

From the Ora Wiki · Updated November 11, 2026
The Ringside
WhereHalfway up the Peak
FightersTwo friends
CrowdPublic duels only
WeaponA week of training

Lore is story, not rules. The real rules are in the articles.

Halfway up the Iron Peak, cut into the rock, is a stone ring called the Ringside. This is where climbers settle friendly rivalries.

Two friends step in from opposite corners, each carrying their own sword: the colors, frame and title they've earned on the climb. When the challenge is accepted, the swords meet in the middle with a crack that echoes down the mountain, and the race is on. The duel isn't fought in the ring itself. It's fought in the week of training that follows.

Some rivals duel in private. Others duel in the open, where any climber can watch from the stone benches and shout their Hype. The crowd never sees how either fighter trains: only who's ahead, and how loudly the benches are cheering.
