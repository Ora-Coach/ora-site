Training

From the Ora Wiki · Updated November 11, 2026
TL;DR
  • A program is what you train. A training block is how hard, phase by phase.
  • Start today's day from Training, or start an empty workout with no program.
  • Lift Check looks over your lifts after each workout. Nothing changes until you open a suggestion and apply it.
  • Test Day is an optional max test: a few warm-ups, then up to 3 heavier singles.
  • Logging your training never needs Pro.

Ora is a training log first. Everything on this page is about using the app, not how it works things out behind the scenes.

Programs#

Workout Plan lists your programs. IN USE marks the one you're on, and Use switches to another. Add a program four ways: pick a ready-made one, build one with Orbi, make your own, or import one from a file. Nothing starts until you tap Start.

Tap a day to edit it, and hold and drag to reorder. In Edit Day, add exercises from the catalog or swipe a card right to remove it. Every change you make to a program can be undone from Plan Changes.

Today's Workout#

Training shows today's day with its exercises, sets and about how long it takes. Tap Start and every set is ready to log. Pick a Different Day swaps in another day, and Not Feeling Well? lets Orbi lighten today. No program? Start an Empty Workout and log as you go.

Logging Sets#

  • 1 2 3 are working sets, W1 is a warm-up and B1 a back-off set.
  • LAST shows last time's set. Tap it to copy it.
  • Tap the check to log a set, and tap again to undo. Rest starts on its own.
  • RPE is optional: how hard the set felt.
  • The ··· menu on an exercise has Swap, Notes, Rest, Superset and Machine.

Training Blocks#

A block plans how hard you train across phases. Tap Plan a Training Block, review it, then Approve Block. Approving applies the first phase only; later phases wait for you. Your own edits always win. Early on, Undo Block puts your program back, and End Block stops it any time with your edits kept.

At each decision point you choose: go to your planned deload, keep the phase longer, end the block, or complete it.

Deloads#

A deload is a planned easy stretch: the same exercises with fewer sets and easier effort, so you start the next block fresh. Ora won't raise your weight or reps on a deload day.

Test Day#

An optional max test, offered after a Peak phase or any time from Test My Max. Ora writes your warm-ups, then you take up to 3 single reps that get heavier, reporting each one's RPE or Missed It. Spotter or safeties on. A pain note or sick day that week turns Test Day off.

Lift Check#

After each workout, Lift Check looks over every lift in your program and sorts them: Needs a Look, Moving, Other Lifts and Left Out (workouts that didn't count, like a sick day). Open it from the Training ••• menu. A suggestion changes nothing until you open it and apply it.

Log Cardio#

On the Cardio page, tap +, pick what you did and add your time. The choices are Treadmill, Outdoor Run, Outdoor Ride, Swim, Bike, Stair Climber, Elliptical, Rower and Other. Distance is optional: miles for a run or ride, yards for a swim. A session of 20 minutes or more counts as a workout for XP, and 30 minutes or more also earns Plan. Each type keeps its own PRs: longer, farther or faster. You can edit or delete it later.

After a Workout#

Rate This Workout takes about 20 seconds and is all optional: how it went, how hard, your energy and what got in the way. Review With Orbi (Pro) talks it through with you.

Common Questions#

Does Ora change my program on its own?

No. Suggestions from Lift Check, blocks and Orbi wait for you to apply them, and every change can be undone.

Do I need a program to use Ora?

No. Start an empty workout and log as you go.

Is Test Day required?

No. It's optional, and you can skip straight to Recover.
