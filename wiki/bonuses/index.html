Bonuses

From the Ora Wiki · Rules updated November 11, 2026
TL;DR
  • Small bonuses for training with friends and groups, and for using a few features.
  • Every bonus needs real training: a counted session or a met weekly goal.
  • All bonuses together add at most 75 Season XP and 300 Level XP a week.
  • Bonuses never count in duels, and nothing Pro-only earns Season XP.
Bonuses
Season XP Cap75 a week
Level XP Cap300 a week
In DuelsNever
Pro-OnlyLevel XP only

Bonuses reward training together and building good habits, in small amounts so they never decide a league.

Your Week's Season XP

Season XP = training (sessions, goal and streak) + duel rewards (up to 100) + bonuses (up to 75). The most in a week is 1,025.

In Detail

Group Week

Your group's Team Quest progress only counts members who share Training Now, but you don't have to share to earn Group Week: you just need to meet your own goal in a week your group completes its quest.

Train Together

The invite only says the day; you don't have to train at the same time or place. Both of you need a counted session that earns Season XP that day, so a 7th training day doesn't qualify.

How the Caps Work

Season XP bonuses add up to at most 75 a week. Level XP bonuses add up in the order you earn them until 300 for the week is used.

Common Questions

Do bonuses help in duels?

No. Duels only count session points.

Can I earn a bonus without training?

No. Every bonus rides on a counted session or a met goal.

Do I need Ora Pro?

Only for Check Your Plan, and it gives Level XP only.
