Bonuses

From the Ora Wiki · Rules updated November 11, 2026
TL;DR
  • Small bonuses for training with friends and groups, and for using a few features.
  • Every bonus needs real training: a counted session or a met weekly goal.
  • Bring a Friend is a one-time +200 Level XP per friend, on top of the weekly cap.
  • All bonuses together add at most 75 Season XP and 300 Level XP a week.
  • Bonuses never count in duels, and nothing Pro-only earns Season XP.

Your Week's Season XP

Season XP = training (sessions, goal and streak) + duel rewards (up to 100) + bonuses (up to 75). The most in a week is 1,025.

In Detail#

Group Week

Your group's Team Quest progress only counts members who share Training Now, but you don't have to share to earn Group Week: you just need to meet your own goal in a week your group completes its quest.

Train Together

The invite only says the day; you don't have to train at the same time or place. Both of you need a counted session that earns Season XP that day, so a 7th training day doesn't qualify.

How the Caps Work

Season XP bonuses add up to at most 75 a week. Level XP bonuses add up in the order you earn them until 300 for the week is used. Duo Week counts toward both caps. Bring a Friend doesn't: it's a one-time lump on top.

Duo Week

Each of your duos pays once a week, when you and that partner both hit your own goals. With 3 duos that's up to +45 Season XP and +135 Level XP, still inside the caps. Only sessions that earned Season XP count. See Duo Streaks.

Bring a Friend

Add Friends shows your friend code (it starts with ORA-) and a Share button. A new account, 7 days old at most, enters one code once: in Add Friends' search or during setup. It can't be changed later, and you can't use your own.

When that friend's counted workouts reach 3, you get +200 Level XP once. It never earns Season XP and never counts in duels. Up to 5 friends pay. Add Friends shows each friend's progress, like "1 of 3 workouts", and your friend sees who invited them.

Worked ExampleGroup Week and Two Train Together Days#

  1. Group Week+40
  2. Train Together, Tuesday+20
  3. Train Together, Thursday+20
  4. Total 80, capped75
  5. Season XP from bonuses+75

Level XP for the same bonuses: 120 + 60 + 60 = 240, under the 300 cap.

Common Questions#

Do bonuses help in duels?

No. Duels only count session points.

Can I earn a bonus without training?

No. Every bonus rides on a counted session or a met goal. For Bring a Friend, it's your friend's first 3 counted workouts.

Does Bring a Friend count toward the 300 cap?

No. It's a one-time +200 Level XP per friend, on top of the weekly cap.

Do I need Ora Pro?

Only for Check Your Plan, and it gives Level XP only.
