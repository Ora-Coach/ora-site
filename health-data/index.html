Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

The health-related data Ora handles, where it comes from, why we use it, who else handles it, and your rights over it.

Effective November 15, 2026

The short version
  • Ora handles health data such as your steps, workouts, body weight, pain notes and readiness answers, to run the app and coach you.
  • It comes from you, and from Apple Health or Health Connect only if you allow it. Ora only reads from them.
  • We never sell your health data and never use it for ads.
  • You can see it, delete it, or withdraw your consent at any time. Email support@oracoach.app and we'll answer within 45 days.

This policy is for people in Washington under the My Health My Data Act, people in Nevada under its consumer health data law (SB 370), and anyone else who uses Ora. It adds to our privacy policy, which explains everything else Ora collects. Ora is made and operated by Orbi Labs LLC ("we").

The Health Data We Collect

  • From Apple Health or Health Connect: steps, active and total calories, distance, workouts and cardio minutes recorded by other apps, and your weight. Ora also uses your phone's motion sensor for live steps.
  • Body details you enter: body weight, height and other measurements, and your age if you choose to give it.
  • Your training: workouts, programs, sets, loads, reps, effort and other training logs.
  • How you're feeling: days you mark as sick or low on energy, and your readiness check-in answers.
  • Pain and injuries: pain and injury notes you log, and the limits you set because of them.

Where It Comes From

  • You: what you enter or log in Ora.
  • Apple Health and Health Connect: only after you give Ora permission, and only what you allow. Ora only reads this data. It never writes to them.

Why We Use It

  • to run Ora's features, such as logging, step goals, next-set targets and your history
  • to coach you, including Orbi's answers and plans if you turn Orbi on
  • to show your progress, records and trends
  • for the optional friends features, only for what you choose to share

We never sell your health data and never use it for ads.

Who Handles It

  • Google Firebase hosts Ora's servers and stores your data, as a service provider that processes it for us. Your health data is stored there if you turn on Cloud Backup, and passes through it for the features below. Steps and other readings from Apple Health or Health Connect are not backed up.
  • Anthropic runs the Claude AI model behind Orbi, Ora's AI coach, and gets data only when you use Orbi or Ora's AI tools. When you message Orbi, it receives your message and recent chat, and your training profile, which can include pain notes and limits, your age and your weight, plus summaries of your recent training and readiness check-ins. Program import, voice commands and workout log import send only the text you give them. Anthropic doesn't train AI models on this data.
  • RevenueCat manages Ora Pro purchases. It receives your Ora account ID and purchase history, never health data.
  • Friends and groups see only what you choose to share, for example your step and cardio totals on a friends scoreboard. You can turn sharing off in Settings, Social.
  • A human coach, only if you enter their invite code. They see a training summary only, never your pain notes, body details or Orbi chats, and you can stop sharing at any time.

We don't sell health data, share it with data brokers, or use it for ads. We may also disclose it if the law requires us to.

How Long We Keep It

Your health data lives on your phone until you delete it or delete your account. Copies in Cloud Backup stay until you delete them or your account. When you delete your account, your data is removed from your phone right away and from Ora's servers shortly after.

Your Rights

  • Access: ask what health data we have about you and who we've shared it with, and get a copy. Settings, Import & Export saves your Ora data as a file.
  • Delete: Settings, Account & Profile, Delete Account deletes your data on your phone and the copies on Ora's servers. You can also ask us by email.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off Ora's Health access in your iPhone's Settings or in Health Connect, turn off Cloud Backup, or stop using Orbi.

How to ask. Email support@oracoach.app from your account email and tell us what you'd like. We may ask you to confirm it's you. We'll answer within 45 days. If we need longer, we'll tell you why within those 45 days.

Appeals. If we turn down your request, reply "Appeal" to our answer and we'll look at it again and tell you what we decided and why. If we deny your appeal, you can contact your state's attorney general, such as the Washington or Nevada attorney general.

We won't discriminate against you for using these rights.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. For big changes, we'll tell you in the app.

Contact
