Privacy overview

Ora privacy policy.

Ora is a fitness, progress, and coaching app in beta. This policy explains what information Ora may collect, why it is used, and how users can request deletion.

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Information Ora may collect

  • Account information: email address, display name, authentication identifiers, and basic account metadata.
  • Training information: programs, exercises, sets, reps, weights, training sessions, effort notes, fatigue notes, and adherence history.
  • Body and progress information: body weight, body metrics, progress notes, check-ins, goals, and user-provided context.
  • Photo and video information: a profile picture you choose, and photos or videos you attach to an exercise during a workout.
  • Activity and health-related information: step count or activity data when a user grants permission through supported device health services such as Health Connect.
  • Coaching and reports: AI-assisted coaching context, report snapshots, coach access grants, coach feedback, and review notes when a user chooses to use those features.
  • Technical information: device type, app version, diagnostics, upload metadata, and security or rate-limit information needed to operate the service.

How Ora uses information

  • To provide workout tracking, progress tracking, reports, reminders, coaching context, and access to Ora's coach web app.
  • To generate evidence-focused summaries, progress views, educational coaching drafts, and optional coach-review packets.
  • To store media you choose to add, such as your profile picture and exercise photos and videos.
  • To improve app reliability, diagnose bugs, prevent abuse, and support account access.
  • To communicate about beta access, app updates, support, or privacy requests.

Former features and their data

Earlier beta versions of Ora offered nutrition logging: meal logs, foods, recipes, calories and macronutrients, meal photos, and food submissions for catalog review. Ora no longer offers these features and no longer collects nutrition information.

  • On your device: the updated app deletes stored nutrition data and the meal and food-label photos it saved.
  • Cloud copies: if you used cloud backup, the updated app deletes your nutrition records from Ora's cloud database after its next backup. Nutrition photos or food submissions that were uploaded to cloud storage, and your references in food submission review records, are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Sooner deletion: you can ask for any remaining nutrition data to be deleted at any time by contacting support@oracoach.app.

Earlier beta versions also offered appearance and physique check-ins, including their photos and measurements. Ora no longer offers these features and no longer collects progress or body photos.

  • On your device: the updated app deletes stored appearance and physique check-ins and their photos.
  • Cloud copies: if you used cloud backup, the updated app deletes these check-ins from Ora's cloud database after its next backup. Check-in photos that were uploaded to cloud storage are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Sooner deletion: you can ask for any remaining appearance or physique data to be deleted at any time by contacting support@oracoach.app.

Earlier beta versions also offered biomechanics capture (movement videos uploaded in Ora's web app and the movement analysis made from them), a body baseline, an injury assessment tool, and a web beta access list. Ora no longer offers these features and no longer collects this information.

  • On your device: the updated app deletes stored body baseline data and photos, injury assessments, and movement comparison results.
  • Cloud copies: biomechanics capture videos, their analysis, your beta access record, and any cloud backup copies of this data are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Sooner deletion: you can ask for any of this data to be deleted at any time by contacting support@oracoach.app.

Camera and media uploads

The Ora app uses photos or videos from your device only when you choose them: a profile picture, or photos and videos you attach to an exercise during a workout. Ora does not collect progress or body photos and does not use the camera in the background.

Health, fitness, and medical boundary

Ora is intended for fitness education, training organization, progress tracking, and coaching support. Ora does not provide nutrition logging or nutrition advice. Ora is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease or injuries. AI-assisted coaching features are educational and approximate unless explicitly reviewed by a qualified professional outside the app.

Sharing and service providers

Ora may use service providers to operate the app, including Google Firebase services for authentication, cloud database storage, file storage, and hosting. These providers process information on Ora's behalf. Ora does not sell personal information.

Users may choose to share report snapshots or coaching context with a coach. Coach access should be explicit, scoped, revocable, and auditable. Ora does not give coaches unrestricted access unless a user or administrator grants access through the product.

Data storage and security

Ora uses reasonable technical safeguards, including encrypted transport where supported by platform services. Some app data may be stored locally on a user's device, and some beta/web features may store data in cloud services. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

Data deletion

You can delete your Ora account in the app: open your account settings and choose Delete Account. After Ora accepts the request, the app removes that account's data from your device; cloud cleanup may take longer. You can also request deletion of Ora account data by contacting support@oracoach.app. Include the email address associated with the account. Some records may be retained where required for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or backup integrity, but Ora will make reasonable efforts to delete user-associated app data from active systems.

Children

Ora is not directed to children. Because Ora involves fitness, body metrics, and progress tracking, it is intended for adult users and testers.

Changes to this policy

Ora may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.

Privacy FAQ

Why does Ora ask for photo access?

So you can choose a profile picture or attach photos and videos to an exercise during a workout. Ora uses only the items you pick, does not collect progress or body photos, and does not use the camera in the background.

Can I request deletion?

Yes. Choose Delete Account in the app's account settings, or email support@oracoach.app from or with the account email and request data deletion.

Does Ora provide medical advice?

No. Ora is for fitness education, tracking, organization, and coaching support. It is not a medical device or diagnostic service.

Does Ora sell personal data?

No. Ora does not sell personal information.

Ora is developed by Eric Zhu for lifters who want training, progress reports, and coach review in one accountable system.
