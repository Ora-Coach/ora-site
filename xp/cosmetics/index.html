Cosmetics

From the Ora Wiki · Updated November 11, 2026
TL;DR
  • Dress up your profile card with a theme, frame, avatar ring and background effect.
  • Most come in sets: on the way to a badge you unlock its effect, then ring, then frame, and the theme when you earn the badge.
  • Rank sets unlock with your level. Season sets come from the season reward track.
  • All avatars are free. Nothing cosmetic needs Pro, and unlocked items stay yours.
Cosmetics
SlotsTheme, frame, ring, effect

Your profile card is what friends, leagues and the Arena see. Everything on it is earned by training.

The Four Slots

Card ThemeThe card's background art and colors.
Card FrameThe border around your card.
Avatar RingThe ring around your avatar.
Background EffectA moving effect behind your card.

Sets on the Way to a Badge

Most badges with a title have a matching set. You don't have to wait for the badge: a quarter of the way there you unlock the set's effect, halfway its ring, three quarters its frame, and the theme comes with the badge itself. The app shows how close you are: "X of N on the way to …".

Example: the Iron Disciple set

Effect at 25 workouts, ring at 50, frame at 75, and the Iron Forge theme with the Dedicated badge at 100.

Season Items

Each season has its own theme, ring, title, frame and effect on its reward track, earned only during that season.

Avatars

Every avatar is free. Pick from five groups: Orbi, Animals, Robots & Space, Heroes and Monsters.

Rank Plaque

Your profile card shows your league tier's emblem on a plaque, from Bronze to Legend. Legend's emblem burns.

Common Questions

Do cosmetics need Pro?

No.

Can I lose an item?

No. Once unlocked, it stays yours.

Do cosmetics give XP?

No. They're just for looks.
