XP

From the Ora Wiki · Rules updated November 11, 2026
TL;DR
  • Every finished workout earns a Session Score of up to 100.
  • Level XP is that score × 3, plus bonuses. It adds up forever and raises your level.
  • Season XP is the weekly score your league ranks you on. It never changes your level.
  • Both are built so every style of training has an equal chance, and rest still counts.

XP is how Ora rewards training. One number, the Session Score, feeds two meters that do different jobs.

Fair for Every Style

At 4 days a week with one PR, a powerlifter (12 sets), a bodybuilder (25 sets) and a calisthenics athlete all earn 505 Season XP. General fitness earns 489 and CrossFit 481. Work tops out, so volume alone doesn't win.

In Detail

What Counts as a Session

A finished workout with at least 3 confirmed working sets or at least 15 minutes of timed work (the time on your timed sets), or a Log Cardio entry of at least 20 minutes. Warm-ups, failed, estimated and unconfirmed sets never count. Sets on an exercise you swapped in do count.

Imported and Health Workouts

Workouts you import from another app (like Strong or Hevy) stay in your history and training stats, but earn no XP, levels or achievements. Workouts Ora reads from Apple Health or Health Connect never count either.

Editing or Deleting a Workout

Ora works your XP out again from your history each time. If you edit a workout so it has fewer sets, or delete it, its XP goes away, and that can lower your level. Adding a workout you forgot to log counts it on the day it happened.

XP From Before Nov 11, 2026

XP you earned under the old rules carried over when the Session Score started, so nobody's level dropped at the switch.

Common Questions

Does Season XP change my level?

No. Season XP only ranks you in your league. Your level comes from Level XP.

Why two kinds of XP?

Levels are a long-term record of your training. Leagues are a weekly race, so they need tighter limits to stay fair.

Is there a level cap?

No. Each level just takes a bit more XP than the last.
