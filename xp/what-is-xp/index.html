XP is how Ora rewards training. Every finished workout earns a Session Score, and that score feeds two separate meters: Level XP, which never resets, and Season XP, which ranks you in your weekly league.

Fair for Every Style

At 4 days a week with one PR, a powerlifter (12 sets), a bodybuilder (25 sets) and a calisthenics athlete all earn 505 Season XP. General fitness earns 489 and CrossFit 481. Work caps out, so volume doesn't win.
