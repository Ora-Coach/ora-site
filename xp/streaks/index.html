Streaks

From the Ora Wiki · Updated November 11, 2026
TL;DR
  • Hit your weekly goal in back-to-back weeks to build a streak. Each week in a row adds +25 Season XP, up to +100.
  • Miss a week and the streak bonus steps down one level instead of resetting.
  • A week with a Sick day, a pain report or a planned deload doesn't step it down.
  • Streak badges count your best run of goal weeks in a row, and any missed week starts a new run.

Missing a Week

If a week ends without your goal, the bonus steps down one level, so one rough week doesn't wipe out a long streak.

What Protects Your Streak

  • Marking a day Sick that week (Low Energy doesn't count).
  • Logging a pain report that week.
  • A planned deload or recovery phase in your training block.

In any of these weeks, a missed goal doesn't step the bonus down.

Streak Badges

The badges On a Roll (2 weeks), Locked In (4), Unbreakable (12) and Year of Iron (52) count your best run of goal weeks in a row. These runs aren't protected: any missed week starts a new run.

Common Questions

Does my streak reset at season end?

No. Your streak is about your weekly goal, not your league.

I was sick. Will my streak drop?

Not if you mark the day Sick.

Is there a streak freeze item?

Not right now. Sick days, pain reports and deloads protect the bonus on their own.
