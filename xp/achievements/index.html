Achievements

From the Ora Wiki · Rules updated November 11, 2026
TL;DR
  • There are 47 achievements (badges) in 9 groups, from your first workout to a 52-week streak, including new ones for your first group, Train Together, duel and rated week.
  • Each one adds Level XP once: Bronze 50, Silver 150, Gold 400, Platinum 1,000, Limited 150.
  • Achievement XP never counts toward Season XP or your league.
  • Some also unlock a title you can show on your profile.

Achievements mark milestones in your training. They count only real, counted workouts, so imported workouts don't unlock them.

In Detail

What Counts Toward Them

"Workouts" means counted sessions (3+ working sets or 15+ minutes of timed work). Sets and tonnage count your working sets only. Plate clubs use your heaviest single rep; kg users see the kg equivalents.

Streak Badges vs the Streak Bonus

Streak badges count your best run of goal weeks in a row; a missed week starts a new run. The weekly streak bonus steps down one level on a miss instead.

Editing History

Ora works achievements out from your history. If you edit or delete the workouts that earned one, it can lock again, and its XP goes with it.

Common Questions

Do achievements count in my league?

No. They add Level XP only.

I missed Halloween week. Can I still get Headless Lifter?

Seasonal badges come back each year, so you can earn it next time.

Do imported workouts count toward badges?

No.
