Achievements

From the Ora Wiki · Rules updated November 11, 2026
Achievements
Bronze+50 XP
Silver+150 XP
Gold+400 XP
Platinum+1,000 XP
Season XPNone

Achievements are badges for milestones like your first PR or a long habit. Each one adds Level XP by its tier, once.

Level Only

Achievement XP raises your level. It never counts toward Season XP or your league.
