TL;DR

Quick answers. Each links to the page with the details.

Common Questions

Why didn't my 40 sets earn more?

Work is full at 8 working sets or 25 minutes of timed work. See Session Score.

Why does my 7th day earn nothing?

Rest is part of training. See Your Week.

Did my level go down when the rules changed?

No. XP from before Nov 11, 2026 carried over. Editing or deleting a workout can lower it, since XP follows your history.

Do imported workouts count?

No. They stay in your history and stats but earn no XP, levels or achievements.

I trained twice today. What counts?

Level XP: the second session earns half, a third nothing. Season XP: only your best session that day.

I changed my weekly goal. Why didn't it change this week?

The goal locks on Monday. Your new goal starts next week.

Does Season XP change my level?

No. Season XP only ranks you in your league.

I was sick. Will I drop a tier?

No. A week with no training doesn't place you in a league. Your streak steps down one level, though. A streak freeze for sickness is coming soon.

Why did I drop a tier at season end?

Everyone drops one tier when a 4-week season ends, never below Bronze.

Are there group or friend bonuses?

Not yet. They're on the Coming Soon page.
