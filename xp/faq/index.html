FAQ

From the Ora Wiki · Rules updated November 11, 2026

Quick answers to what players ask most.

Why didn't my 40 sets earn more?

Work is full at 8 working sets or 25 minutes of timed work. More sets add nothing, so every style has an equal chance.

Why does my 7th day earn nothing?

Rest is part of training. Days 5 and 6 count half for Season XP, and a 7th training day earns nothing in either meter.

Did my level go down?

No. XP earned before Nov 11, 2026 carried over, so nobody's level dropped.

Do imported workouts count?

No. Workouts from Strong or Hevy stay in your history and stats, but don't earn XP.

I trained twice today. What counts?

Level XP: the second session earns half, a third nothing. Season XP: only your best session that day.

I set 5 PRs. Why only 15 points?

One PR earns the full Progress points. More PRs in the same session add nothing.

Does Season XP change my level?

No. Season XP only ranks you in your league.

Why did I drop a tier at season end?

Everyone drops one tier when a 4-week season ends, never below Bronze.
