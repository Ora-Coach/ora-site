Titles & Plates

From the Ora Wiki · Updated November 11, 2026
TL;DR
  • A title shows under your name on your profile, in leagues and in duels. Each one has its own plate.
  • Rank titles come with your level, from Newbie at level 1 to Olympus at 50.
  • Most other titles come from an achievement. Seasonal ones come back every year.
  • League Season titles can only be earned during their season, and appear here when it starts.
  • Your highest rank shows by default. Tap any title you own to show it instead.

Titles are how other lifters know what you've done. Each plate is drawn to match its title.

League Seasons

Each season has a title on its reward track. In Iron Autumn it's Iron Disciple at 825 Season XP, and you can also earn it from the Dedicated achievement. New season titles are added here when their season starts.

Lore

Why Climbers Wear Titles

On the Iron Peak, nobody wears a crown for long, so climbers wear titles instead: small plates carved at the Forge, each one a story of something done. Ask a climber about their plate and you'll get the story whether you wanted it or not.

Common Questions

Can I lose a title?

Titles you unlock stay yours. If you edit or delete the workouts that earned its achievement, the achievement and its title can lock again.

Can I show any title?

Any title you own. Your highest rank shows until you pick one.

Where are the League Season titles?

Each one is added when its season starts.
