Orbi

From the Ora Wiki · Updated November 11, 2026
TL;DR
  • Orbi is Ora's coach and its only AI. It's part of Ora Pro and only runs after you turn it on.
  • Ask about a swap, a cue, how hard to go or how your week went, or have Orbi draft a program.
  • Orbi drafts, you decide: nothing changes until you tap Apply, and every change has Undo.
  • Your data isn't used to train AI, and Orbi never sees your gym location.
  • Pick a voice: Classic, Gen Z, Hype Bro, Bestie, Old-School Coach or Science Nerd.

Orbi lives in the Coach tab and shows up across the app with a tip, a cheer or a plan. Orbi keeps it short and never pretends a guess is a fact.

What You Can Ask

  • In chat: a swap, a cue, how your week went, or anything about your training. Type / for shortcuts, or tap + to attach today's plan, a workout or a lift.
  • Today's Card: start today's workout from it, or tell Orbi how you feel.
  • In a workout: Ask Orbi Anything about a swap, a cue or how hard to go.
  • On a program: Audit Program, Improve with Coach, or Build the Rest to fill out your week from the days you made.
  • Social: Orbi can draft a friend request, nudge or cheer for you to confirm.

Orbi Drafts, You Decide

When Orbi suggests a change, it waits as a card. Review it; nothing changes until you tap Apply. Every applied change shows up in Plan Changes with Undo.

What Orbi Won't Do

Orbi explains your weight targets but doesn't set them, and stays out of nutrition, payments, your account, consent, exporting data and deleting history. For those, Orbi opens the right page so you can do it yourself. Orbi doesn't diagnose or treat pain or injuries.

Your Privacy

  • The AI provider doesn't train on your data.
  • Orbi never sees your gym location.
  • Orbi's Notebook only remembers what you confirm, and you can correct or delete any note.
  • Without Orbi turned on, everything runs on your phone with no AI requests.
  • Orbi's answers help, but they aren't verified facts.

Free and Pro

Logging never needs Pro. Pro adds Orbi: a Smart Program that changes with how your training actually goes, a Weekly Check-In, and an Orbi that remembers your goals, injuries and preferences.

Lore

Where Orbi Came From

When the first climber at the Foothill Forge brought a bar down on the anvil, a spark flew out and didn't land. It hovered, blinked, and asked what they were training today. That spark was Orbi.

Common Questions

Can Orbi change my workouts without asking?

No. Orbi only changes things when you tap Apply on a change it drafted, and you can undo it.

Can I turn Orbi off?

Yes, from the gear in Coach. Everything else keeps working.

Does Orbi know where my gym is?

No.
