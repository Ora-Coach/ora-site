Your Week

From the Ora Wiki · Rules updated November 11, 2026
TL;DR
  • Your week runs Monday to Sunday in your own time zone.
  • For Season XP, training days 1–4 count in full, days 5–6 count half and day 7 counts nothing.
  • Hit the weekly goal you set (1–6 days) for a bonus: 50 to 250 Season XP.
  • Hit it again next week to build a streak: +25 a week, up to +100. A sick, pain or deload week freezes it.

In Detail

"Day 1" Means Your First Training Day

The days count in the order you train, not by weekday. If you train Wednesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday, those are days 1–4 and all count in full.

Two Sessions in One Day

Season XP takes only your best session that day. For Level XP, the second session that day earns half and a third earns nothing.

The 7th Day

A 7th training day in one week earns no Season XP and no Level XP. It still counts for your history, achievements and toward your goal.

The Goal Locks on Monday

Changing your goal mid-week applies from next Monday. This week keeps the goal it started with, so you can't lower it on Friday to hit it. A goal of 7 days pays like 6.

How the Streak Steps

Your first goal week pays +0; the streak bonus starts on the 2nd week in a row. Each week that ends without hitting your goal steps the streak down one level (for example from +75 to +50), not to zero. A week with no training at all counts as a miss, unless it's frozen.

Streak Freeze

A missed goal week doesn't step your streak down if, on any day that week, you:

  • marked a day Sick (through Not Feeling Well? or History; Low Energy doesn't freeze),
  • logged and confirmed a pain report, or
  • were in a training block's deload or recovery phase (for up to 14 days from when it started).

A freeze only stops the step down; it doesn't add a goal week. It reads what's logged on this phone.

Worked ExampleA 4-Day Goal, 5 Days Trained, 3rd Goal Week in a Row

  1. Day 1: 85 · Day 2: 100 (a PR) · Day 3: 85 · Day 4: 85355
  2. Day 5: 85 counts half+42
  3. Goal of 4 met+150
  4. Streak, 3rd week in a row+50
  5. Season XP this week597

Level XP for the same week: (85 + 100 + 85 + 85 + 85) × 3 = 1,320, plus 450 for the goal and 150 for the streak, so 1,920.

Common Questions

Why does my 7th day earn nothing?

Rest is part of training. Days 5 and 6 count half for Season XP, and a 7th day earns nothing in either meter.

I changed my goal. Why didn't this week change?

The goal locks on Monday. Your new goal starts next week.

I missed a week. Did I lose my streak?

Not all of it. It steps down one level, or not at all if you logged being sick, a pain report or a deload that week.

Can I set a 7-day goal?

You can, but it pays like a 6-day goal, since a 7th day earns nothing.
