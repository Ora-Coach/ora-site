Your Week

From the Ora Wiki · Rules updated November 11, 2026
Your Week
Full Days1–4
Half Days5–6
Day 7Rest (0)
Goal Range1–6 days (7 = 6)
Goal LocksMonday
Streak Max+100 a week

Your week runs Monday to Sunday. Training days earn less as they add up, so rest is part of the plan. Hit the weekly goal you set for a bonus, and keep hitting it for a streak.
