Patch Notes

From the Ora Wiki · Rules updated November 11, 2026
TL;DR
  • Fair XP Update, live November 11, 2026.
  • The Session Score (up to 100) replaced per-set XP, so high volume no longer wins.
  • Later the same day: cardio sessions, hold and cardio PRs, the streak freeze, bonuses, four new badges and public duels.
  • Levels carried over; Season XP and tiers started fresh at the switch.

DateNov 11, 2026
LevelsCarried over

What changed on November 11, 2026.

  • NewThe Session Score (up to 100) replaces the old per-set XP.
  • ChangedWork is full at 8 working sets or 25 minutes of timed work.
  • ChangedSeason XP: days 1–4 full, 5–6 half, day 7 none. Weekly most is 850, plus 100 from duels.
  • ChangedLevel XP is the Session Score × 3; a 7th training day earns nothing.
  • ChangedDuels count only session points earned after both accept. Rewards are capped at 100 a week.
  • FixedSets on a swapped-in exercise now count.
  • ChangedSeason reward track: 225 / 500 / 825 / 1,150 / 1,550.
  • NewLog Cardio sessions of 20+ minutes count as sessions (up to 70, or 85 with a cardio PR).
  • NewPRs for longer timed holds and for cardio (time, distance or pace).
  • NewStreak freeze: a sick day, a pain report or a deload or recovery phase keeps a missed week from stepping your streak down.
  • NewBonuses: Group Week, Train Together, Rate Your Workout and Check Your Plan, capped at 75 Season XP and 300 Level XP a week.
  • NewFour Bronze badges: First Group, First Train Together, First Duel and First Rated Week.
  • NewPublic duels: watch and Hype friends' duels in the Arena.
  • NoteLevels carried over. Season XP and tiers reset to Bronze at the switch, open duels were cancelled, and rewards already earned stayed.

Common Questions

Why the change?

The old XP gave people who did more sets a big advantage. Now every style gets an equal chance, and rest is part of training.
