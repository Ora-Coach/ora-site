Social

From the Ora Wiki · Updated November 11, 2026
TL;DR
  • Add friends, join groups and cheer each other on. Social is optional.
  • Friends see that you trained, never your sets, weights or location, unless you choose to share more.
  • Train Together: invite a friend for a day; you don't have to be in the same gym.
  • Public duels show up in the Arena, where anyone can watch and Hype a side.
  • Everything is switchable in Settings › Social.

Ora is better with friends, but nothing about your training is public unless you say so.

Friends and Your Circle

Find people with Search. Your Inbox holds friend requests, nudges, cheers and invites. Rings on your circle show who's training now (green), who's done today (gold) and who has Ora open (a dot).

Activity

Friends' workouts, PRs, level-ups and new titles show in your feed. React with one of six reactions, and tap yours again to take it back.

Nudges and High Fives

Nudge a friend who hasn't trained, or High Five one who trained the same day as you. Nudges are limited so nobody gets spammed.

Groups and Team Quests

Create a group or join one with an invite code. Each week the group works toward a Team Quest, a shared number of workouts.

Training Now

When you start a workout, your circle can see that you're training, then that you're done. Never your sets, weights or location.

Train Together

Invite a friend for a day. They answer Count Me In or Not This Time. If you both train that day, you both get a bonus.

Duels and the Arena

Challenge a friend to a duel. If you both choose Public, it shows in the Arena tab in Ranks, where anyone can watch and Hype a side. Only names, looks and the duel score show.

Your Sharing Switches

Settings › Social has a switch for each of these: Show Training Now, Show When I'm Online, Allow Public Duels, Show Workout Name, Show Workout Length and Share Highlights, plus Quiet Hours and a switch for each kind of notification. You can block or mute anyone, and turn off Show Me in Leagues to leave leagues.

Common Questions

Can friends see my weights?

No. They see that you trained, and your workout name and length only if you turn those on.

Do I have to use social features?

No. Ora works fully on your own.

Can strangers see my duels?

Only public ones, and only if both of you chose Public.
