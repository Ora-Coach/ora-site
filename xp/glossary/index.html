Glossary

From the Ora Wiki · Updated November 11, 2026
TL;DR
  • Short definitions of the terms Ora uses, A to Z.
  • Each links back to the full article where there is one.
Glossary
Terms21
OrderA to Z

The words you'll see in Ora, in plain language.

Back-Off Set (B1)A lighter set after your heaviest one.
BlockA training block: a plan for how hard you train, phase by phase.
Build, Push, PeakBlock phases. Build adds work, Push gets heavier, Peak gets you ready to show your strength.
Counted SessionA workout that earns XP: 3+ working sets, 15+ minutes of timed work, or 20+ minutes of cardio.
DeloadA planned easy stretch: the same exercises with fewer sets and easier effort.
Est.Ora's estimate of your max for a lift.
HypeA cheer for one side of a public duel. It gives no points.
Level XPXP that raises your level. It never goes away for resting.
Lift CheckOra's look over your lifts after each workout.
PRPersonal record: your best so far. Ora tracks heaviest weight, estimated max, rep records, longest hold, and cardio bests.
ProgramWhat you train: your days, exercises and sets.
RPEHow hard a set felt, out of 10. RPE 8 means about 2 reps left.
Recover, BaseBlock phases. Recover is a deload; Base is general training between blocks.
RotationOne pass through all your program days, however long it takes.
Season XPXP that ranks you in your league each week.
Session ScoreYour score for one workout, out of 100.
Team QuestA group's shared weekly workout goal.
Test DayAn optional max test after a Peak phase.
Top SetYour heaviest set of the day for a lift.
Warm-Up Set (W1)A lighter set before your working sets.
Working SetA real set that counts, numbered 1, 2, 3.
