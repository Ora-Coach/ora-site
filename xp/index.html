XP Guide

How XP Works

Every workout in Ora earns a Session Score. Here's how it adds up to your level, your league and your duels, and why it's fair to every way of training.

Rules live since November 11, 2026.

The Short Version
  • Each finished workout earns a Session Score of up to 100: 40 for showing up, 30 for the work, 15 for following your plan and 15 for a PR.
  • The score tops out at 8 working sets or 25 minutes of timed work, so more volume doesn't earn more.
  • Hit your weekly goal for a bonus, and keep hitting it for a growing streak bonus.
  • Level XP is your Session Score × 3, plus bonuses and achievements. Leagues never change it.
  • Season XP ranks you in your league. Your first 4 training days count in full, days 5 and 6 count half, and day 7 is for rest.

What Counts as a Session

A workout earns XP when you finish it and it has at least 3 working sets or at least 15 minutes of timed work (timed sets like holds, intervals or a conditioning block).

Working sets you logged and confirmedWarm-up sets

Timed work you logged in the workoutWorkouts imported from other apps (they stay in your history and stats)

The Session Score

Every counted session gets a score out of 100, built from four parts. Showing up is worth the most, because consistency is what builds results.

Work: Full at 8 Sets or 25 Minutes

Work fills up as you train and is full at 8 working sets or 25 minutes of timed work, whichever you reach first. Past that, extra sets earn nothing more. A 12-set strength day and a 25-set hypertrophy day both get the full 30.

Plan: Do What You Set Out to Do

On a program day, finish at least 80% of the planned sets. On a free workout, train for at least 30 minutes with at least 6 working sets. Either way, it's 15 points.

Progress: One PR Is Enough

Set at least one personal record and you get 15 points. Ora counts a PR when you hit a new best estimated one-rep max, lift your heaviest weight on an exercise, or do more reps than ever at a weight (or heavier). Bodyweight reps count too. One PR earns the full 15, and more PRs in the same session add nothing, so there's no reason to chase small records on every accessory.

Your Week: Rest Is Part of Training

For Season XP, your best session each day counts, and your training days in a week (Monday to Sunday) earn less as they add up. Your first 4 training days count in full, the 5th and 6th count half, and a 7th counts nothing. Training more still pays, but never so much that skipping rest looks like a good idea.

Weekly Goal and Streak

You choose how many days a week you plan to train, from 1 to 6. (A 7-day goal counts as 6.) Hit it and you earn a bonus that grows with the goal. Your goal locks for the week on Monday, so changing it mid-week doesn't change this week's target.

Level XP

Your level is the long game. Level XP adds up from:

Each counted sessionSession Score × 3 (up to 300)

Weekly goal metGoal bonus × 3 (150 to 750)
StreakStreak bonus × 3 (up to 300)
AchievementsBronze 50, Silver 150, Gold 400, Platinum 1,000

Level n needs 50 × n × (n + 1) total XP. Each level takes a bit longer than the last.

All the XP you earned before these rules started on November 11, 2026 carried over, so nobody's level went down.

Season XP

Season XP is a separate weekly score that ranks you in your league. It never changes your level. Each week it adds up:

  • Your best session each day, by its Session Score: training days 1 to 4 in full, days 5 and 6 at half, day 7 nothing (most: 500).
  • Your goal bonus and streak bonus, not multiplied (most: 250 + 100).
  • Duels, up to 100 more.

So the most anyone can earn in a week is 850, plus up to 100 from duels.

Leagues and Tiers

Each week you compete with about 30 members in your tier. Your week runs Monday to Sunday in your own time zone, and results come out for everyone on Monday at 9 AM Eastern.

Duels

A duel is a 1-on-1 race with a friend inside one league week. Your duel score is the session points you each earn after you both accept. Goal and streak bonuses don't count, so it's purely about the training you do from that point.

Fair for Every Style of Training

Powerlifters do a few heavy sets. Bodybuilders do lots of sets. Calisthenics athletes count reps and holds, CrossFit athletes work against the clock, and plenty of people just want to stay healthy. Ora scores showing up, doing the work and following your plan, and the Work part fills up at a level every style reaches. So at the same number of training days, everyone lands close together.

Why It's Built This Way

  • Every style gets an equal chance. Scoring caps out at 8 working sets or 25 minutes, so high-volume training doesn't outscore heavy, low-volume or timed training. Your score comes from your own plan and your own records, not from how much weight anyone else lifts.
  • Showing up matters most. Show Up is the biggest part of every score, because consistency over weeks is what builds strength and health.
  • Rest is part of training. Extra days are worth less and a 7th day is worth nothing, so climbing never means skipping recovery.
  • No tricks pay off. Splitting one workout into several, piling on junk sets, or chasing tiny PRs on every exercise doesn't earn more.

Common Questions

Why didn't my 40 sets earn more?

Work is full at 8 working sets (or 25 minutes of timed work). Past that, more sets earn nothing extra. That keeps a 40-set session from outscoring a focused 10-set session, so every style of training has an equal chance.

Why does my 7th day earn nothing?

Rest is part of training. In Season XP your 5th and 6th training days count half and a 7th counts nothing, and in Level XP a 7th training day in a week earns nothing. Your weekly goal still counts that day toward the goal.

Did my level go down when the rules changed?

No. All the XP you earned before November 11, 2026 carried over, so nobody's level dropped.

Do imported workouts count?

No. Workouts imported from other apps like Strong or Hevy stay in your history and training stats, but they don't earn XP.

Do warm-up sets count?

No. Only working sets you logged and confirmed count. Warm-ups, failed sets and estimated or unconfirmed sets don't.

I swapped an exercise. Do those sets count?

Yes. Sets you log on a swapped-in exercise count like any other working sets.

I trained twice today. What happens?

For Level XP, your second session earns half and a third earns nothing. For Season XP, only your best session of the day counts.

I set 5 PRs today. Why only 15 points?

One PR earns the full Progress points. More PRs in the same session don't add more, so there's no reason to chase small records on every exercise.

I missed a week. Did I lose my streak?

Not all of it. A missed week steps your streak down one level instead of resetting it to zero, so one off week doesn't undo months of consistency.

Why did I drop a tier when the season ended?

Everyone drops one tier at the end of each 4-week season (never below Bronze). It gives every season a fresh climb.
