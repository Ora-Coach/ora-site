Session Score

From the Ora Wiki · Rules updated November 11, 2026
TL;DR
  • A counted workout scores up to 100: Show Up 40, Work 30, Plan 15, Progress 15.
  • A Log Cardio session of 20+ minutes counts too: up to 70, or 85 with a cardio PR.
  • Work is full at 8 working sets or 25 minutes of timed work. More earns nothing.
  • Plan pays when you finish 80% of a program day, or do 30+ minutes and 6+ sets on a free workout.
  • One PR in the session earns the full Progress points. Holds and cardio can set PRs.

Cardio Sessions

A Log Cardio entry (treadmill or machine) of 20 minutes or more counts as a session on its own. It scores Show Up 40, Work by its minutes (full at 25), no Plan, and Progress 15 for a cardio PR.

In Detail

Timed Work

Timed minutes are the time on your timed sets (holds, intervals, a conditioning block), not how long the workout was open. Ora uses whichever is higher, sets or minutes, so mixing them never hurts.

Plan on a Program Day

Ora compares the working sets you finished with the sets planned for that day. 8 of 10 planned (80%) earns Plan; 7 of 10 doesn't. Extra sets beyond the plan count as done.

Plan on a Free Workout

The workout must run at least 30 minutes from start to finish and have at least 6 working sets.

What Counts as a PR

Ora's one PR rule. An exercise's first workout is its starting point, and a tie beats nothing.

  • Lifts: a higher estimated one-rep max, a heavier weight, or more reps at that weight or heavier. Bodyweight reps count.
  • Timed holds: a longer hold than ever at the same load or heavier.
  • Cardio (each type on its own, like treadmill or rower): a longer time than any earlier session, a longer distance, or a faster pace than every earlier session within ±10% of this distance.
  • Conditioning blocks don't have a PR type yet.

Warm-up, failed, unconfirmed and pain-limited sets never set a PR.

Cardio in Detail

Work is 30 × minutes ÷ 25, rounded down, so 20 minutes is 24 and 25 minutes or more is the full 30. Cardio has no Plan points, so a cardio session tops out at 85. A cardio log inside a lifting workout adds nothing to that workout's score. A treadmill entry's distance is its speed × time. Cardio from Apple Health, Health Connect or imports never counts.

Sets That Don't Count

Warm-ups, failed sets, estimated sets and sets you didn't confirm. They stay in your log; they just don't score.

Common Questions

Why didn't my 40 sets earn more?

Work is full at 8 working sets. Past that, sets add nothing, so a 40-set day and a 10-set day both get 30.

I set 5 PRs today. Why only 15 points?

One PR earns the full Progress points. More PRs in the same session add nothing.

Does a run or bike ride count?

Yes, if you log it with Log Cardio and it lasts 20 minutes or more. Cardio from Apple Health or Health Connect doesn't count.

Can a plank set a PR?

Yes. A longer hold than ever at the same load or heavier is a PR.

Does a really short workout count?

Yes, if it has 3 working sets or 15 minutes of timed work. It earns Show Up and a bit of Work.
