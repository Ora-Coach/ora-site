Season Chronicles

From the Ora Wiki · Updated November 11, 2026

Every season on the Iron Peak gets a chapter. New chapters are added when their season begins.

Lore is story, not rules. The real rules are in the articles.

Chapter One · Fall 2026

Iron Autumn

The first season on the Iron Peak opened in the fall, when the Forge burned hotter than it ever had. The old smiths called it Iron Autumn.

For four weeks the camps raced. Those who climbed far enough took home pieces of the Forge itself: its colors, a ring cast from a year of iron, the title of Iron Disciple, the frame of the Strength Warlord and, for the very few, the mark of the Mountain Mover.

When the four weeks ended, the winds came as they always do, and every climber slid down one camp to start again. Nobody minded much. They had their rewards, and they knew the way up now.
