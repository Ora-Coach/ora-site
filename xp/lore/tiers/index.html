The Six Camps

From the Ora Wiki · Updated November 11, 2026

Six camps sit on the way up the Iron Peak, one for each tier.

Lore is story, not rules. The real rules are in the articles.

Bronze Camp

The Foothill Forge

“Everyone starts here.”

At the foot of the Iron Peak burns the Foothill Forge, where every climber hammers out their first plates. The fires never go out, and nobody is ever turned away. The winds that sweep the mountain each season can push a climber down, but never below the Forge: the foothills hold everyone who has ever started.

Silver Camp

Silverstream

“Show up again.”

A cold river runs past the second camp, and climbers say it tests one thing: whether you come back tomorrow. The ones who reach Silverstream learned that a single great day matters less than the next ordinary one.

Gold Camp

The Gilded Steps

“Earned, not given.”

Nobody built the Gilded Steps. Climbers wore them into the rock, one week at a time, until the stone shone. Every step is someone's best week. Somewhere in there is one of yours.

Platinum Camp

The Glacier Halls

“Steady in the cold.”

Above the tree line the air thins and the ice begins. The Glacier Halls belong to the steady: climbers who train the same way in a blizzard as on a sunny morning. The ice punishes rushing and rewards patience.

Diamond Camp

The Crystal Ridge

“Pressure makes us.”

The ridge glitters because it was crushed. Old climbers say the mountain squeezed coal here for a thousand years until it turned to diamond, and that the climbers who reach it went through something similar.

Legend Camp

The Summit

“The summit remembers.”

Few have stood at the top of the Iron Peak, and the ones who have rarely talk about it. There is a crown up there, they say, but it never stays on one head for long. The summit doesn't keep anyone. It only remembers who made the climb.
