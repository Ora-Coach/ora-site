Leagues & Tiers

From the Ora Wiki · Rules updated November 11, 2026
TL;DR
  • Each week you race about 30 lifters in your rank on Season XP.
  • Results land Monday at 9 AM Eastern: the top 5 move up, the bottom 5 drop. Smaller leagues move fewer.
  • Six tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond and Legend. Bronze never drops.
  • Just moved up? You can't drop the next week. A rest week never drops you.

Each week you race about 30 lifters in your rank. Finish near the top to climb. Smaller leagues move fewer than 5. Your Season XP for the week is your training, plus duel rewards (up to 100) and bonuses (up to 75).

Results Every Monday

Your week runs Monday to Sunday. Results land Monday at 9 AM ET: the top 5 move up, the bottom 5 drop.

Safety Nets

Just moved up? You can't drop the next week. A rest week never drops you: you join a week's league with your first workout.

In Detail

Smaller Leagues

A full league moves 5 up and 5 down. A smaller league moves fewer people each way, and a very small league has no drops.

Moving Up Needs Season XP

You only move up with Season XP above 0. In Legend, finishing in the top places earns the crown instead.

Ties

Equal Season XP is broken by who reached that score first.

Protection After Moving Up

The week after you move up, you can't drop, even in the bottom places. The drop slot isn't passed to anyone else. Season end still drops everyone one tier.

Rest Weeks

You join a week's league with your first workout that earns Season XP. A week with no training doesn't place you, so it can't drop you.

Quiet Tiers

When a tier is quiet, it can race with the tier next to it for that week, so every league has enough people.

Fair Play

Unusual activity can hold your tier in place for a week while it's checked. You don't move up or down that week.

Common Questions

Does my league affect my level?

No. Season XP and leagues never change your level.

I was sick all week. Will I drop?

No. A week with no training doesn't place you in a league, so you can't drop.

Who are the people in my league?

About 30 members of your tier, including people you don't know. They see your name, username, avatar, level, title and Season XP, not your workouts.

When do results come out?

Monday at 9 AM Eastern, for everyone.
