Leagues & Tiers

From the Ora Wiki · Rules updated November 11, 2026
Leagues
Gold League
Tiers6
Group SizeAbout 30
WeekMon–Sun, your time zone
ResultsMonday 9 AM ET
Move UpTop 5
DropBottom 5

Each week you race about 30 lifters in your rank. Finish near the top to climb. Smaller leagues move fewer than 5.

  1. LegendFinish top 5 in DiamondTop 5 · CrownBottom 5
  2. DiamondFinish top 5 in PlatinumTop 5 → LegendBottom 5
  3. PlatinumFinish top 5 in GoldTop 5 → DiamondBottom 5
  4. GoldYou · #5Finish top 5 in SilverTop 5 → PlatinumBottom 5
  5. SilverFinish top 5 in BronzeTop 5 → GoldBottom 5
  6. BronzeWhere everyone startsTop 5 → SilverNo Drops
Results Every Monday

Your week runs Monday to Sunday. Results land Monday at 9 AM ET: the top 5 move up, the bottom 5 drop.

Safety Nets

Just moved up? You can't drop the next week. A rest week never drops you: you join a week's league with your first workout.
