The Bonus Caps

All bonuses together add at most 75 Season XP and 300 Level XP a week. Nothing that needs Ora Pro will earn Season XP, so paying never wins a league. Bonuses never count in duels.

Rule Changes

Streak Freeze Coming Soon · not in the app yet

A week you log as sick, in pain or a planned break won't step your streak down, so nobody trains through illness to protect it.

Cardio Sessions Coming Soon · not in the app yet

A logged cardio session of 20 minutes or more (a run, a ride, a swim) will count as a session on its own.

New PR Types Coming Soon · not in the app yet

A longer hold, a faster time or more rounds on a repeated conditioning block, and a longer or faster cardio session will count as PRs for Progress.

Public Duels in the Arena Coming Soon · not in the app yet

Duels other members can watch, in a new Arena.

In Detail

Why the Caps Are Small

75 Season XP is less than one full session. Members who train alone or keep things private are never more than 75 behind in a league.
