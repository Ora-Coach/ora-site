Levels

From the Ora Wiki · Rules updated November 11, 2026
TL;DR
  • Level n needs 50 × n × (n + 1) total Level XP: Level 2 is 300, Level 10 is 5,500.
  • Each level takes 100 XP more than the one before.
  • A member who trains 3 days a week reaches about Level 10 in 5 weeks and Level 30 in about 8 months.
  • There's no cap.

In Detail

How "Typical" Is Worked Out

Three sessions a week, each scoring 85 (Show Up 40, full Work 30, Plan 15, no PR), a 3-day goal hit every week and the streak growing to +100. That's 1,065 Level XP in week 1, rising to 1,365 a week from week 5. Achievements come on top, so most members get there a little sooner.

Can My Level Go Down?

Not from leagues, duels or rest. Ora works your XP out from your history, so editing a workout to remove sets, or deleting it, takes its XP away and can lower your level.

The Most Level XP in a Week

About 2,850 before achievements: 6 sessions × 300, plus 750 for a 6-day goal and 300 for a full streak.

Common Questions

Did my level go down when the rules changed?

No. XP from before Nov 11, 2026 carried over.

Do imported workouts help my level?

No. Imported workouts earn no XP.

Does Season XP add to my level?

No. They're separate.
