Duels

From the Ora Wiki · Rules updated November 11, 2026
Duels
Players1v1 with a friend
LengthOne league week
Winner+100 Season XP
Tie+50 each
Loser+20 if they trained
Weekly Cap100

Challenge a friend to a 1v1 inside one league week. Only the session points you each earn after both of you accept count. Goal and streak bonuses don't.

YouSession points after accepting
Your FriendSession points after accepting
ResultSeason XP
Win+100
Tie+50 each
Lose, trained after accepting+20
Lose, didn't train0
Level Stays Put

Duel rewards are Season XP only, capped at 100 a week. They never change your level.
