Duels

From the Ora Wiki · Rules updated November 11, 2026
TL;DR
  • A duel is a 1v1 race with a friend inside one league week.
  • Your score is the session points you each earn after the duel is accepted. Goal and streak bonuses don't count.
  • Win +100 Season XP, tie +50 each, lose +20 if you trained after accepting.
  • Duel rewards are capped at 100 a week and never change your level.

In Detail

Sending and Accepting

You can send a duel Monday through Thursday, until 11:59 PM your time. Your friend has 24 hours to accept, or until the week ends if that comes first. You can duel the same friend once a week, and have up to 3 duels pending or running at once.

Who You Can Duel

Friends who have accepted your friend request, with no block either way, and who are both in leagues.

How the Score Works

When the duel is accepted, Ora notes each side's session points for the week. The score is what each of you adds after that. Goal and streak bonuses never count.

When a Duel Doesn't Count

If neither of you trains after accepting, the duel is void and nobody earns anything. Removing the friend or blocking also voids it, as does a flagged week on either side.

Ties

A tie needs exactly equal scores. Each side that trained gets +50.

The Cap

Duel rewards add at most 100 Season XP a week, and never more than the Season XP you earned from training that week. Win two duels and you still get 100.

  2. Your friend: 100 at accepting, 285 on Sunday185

  4. Your friend trained after accepting+20 Season XP

Common Questions

Do duels change my level?

No. Duel rewards are Season XP only.

Can I duel someone who isn't my friend?

Not yet. Public duels are coming soon.

I won but only earned 60. Why?

Duel rewards can't be more than the Season XP you earned from training that week.

Can I send a duel on Friday?

No. Sending closes Thursday at 11:59 PM your time.
