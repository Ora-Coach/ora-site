Seasons

From the Ora Wiki · Rules updated November 11, 2026
TL;DR
  • A season is 4 league weeks.
  • Your Season XP adds up across the 4 weeks and unlocks rewards at 225, 500, 825, 1,150 and 1,550.
  • When a season ends, everyone drops one tier (Bronze stays Bronze), so each season is a fresh climb.
  • Rewards you unlock are yours to keep.

In Detail

What Adds to Your Season Total

Each week's Season XP, including duel rewards, adds to your season total once that week's results are in.

Season End

After the last week's results, everyone drops one tier, never below Bronze. Protection from moving up doesn't apply here. Your season total starts again at 0.

Between Seasons

When no season is running, there's no league and no results that week.

Season Items

Each season has its own items (theme, ring, title, frame, effect). They can only be earned during their season.

Common Questions

Why did I drop a tier when the season ended?

Everyone drops one tier at season end, never below Bronze.

Do I keep my rewards?

Yes. Rewards you unlock stay yours.

Does my streak reset with the season?

No. Your streak is about your weekly goal, not the league.
