Founder

EZ

Eric Zhu

Founder & CEO, Orbi Labs LLC

Eric is a computer science student at the University of Florida. He started lifting in his senior year of high school, and since a real coach was always too expensive, he set out to build the coach he wished he had. Ora started as a club project at UF, and when the team fell away he kept going on his own.

Today he designs, builds and runs Ora: the iPhone app, Orbi the AI coach, the backend and this website.
