Dumbbell Lateral Raise

The lateral raise lifts dumbbells out to your sides. It isolates the side delts, the muscle that makes shoulders look wider.

Main musclesSide delts
EquipmentDumbbells
TypeShoulder isolation

Muscles worked

MainSide delts
HelpingUpper trapsFront delts

How to do it

  1. Stand tall with a light dumbbell in each hand at your sides, palms facing in.
  2. Keep a small bend in your elbows.
  3. Raise your arms out to the sides, leading with your elbows.
  4. Stop when your arms are about level with your shoulders.
  5. Lower slowly back to your sides.

Common mistakes

  • Going too heavy. Swinging the weight up takes the work off the side delts. Pick a weight you can lift with control.
  • Shrugging. Lifting the shoulders turns it into a trap exercise. Keep them down.
  • Raising too far forward. Keep the dumbbells out to the sides, slightly in front of your body.

Sets and reps

  • Muscle: 3-4 sets of 10-20 reps, stopping 1-3 reps short of failure.
  • Light weights work well here. Add reps before adding weight.

Questions

What muscles does the lateral raise work?

Mainly the side (lateral) delts, with the upper traps and front delts helping.

How high should I raise the dumbbells?

About shoulder height. Going higher shifts the work to the traps.

How heavy should lateral raises be?

Light. Most people use far less weight here than in any press. If you have to swing it, it is too heavy.
