Bulgarian Split Squat

A single-leg squat with your back foot up on a bench. It builds the quads and glutes with far less weight than a back squat.

How to Do It

1
Set Your FeetStand about two feet in front of a bench with the top of your back foot on it.
2
Hold the WeightsA dumbbell in each hand at your sides.
3
LowerStraight down until your front thigh is about parallel and your back knee nearly touches the floor.
4
Stay BalancedFront heel down, front knee in line with your toes.
5
Stand UpPush through your front foot.
6
Switch LegsFinish all reps on one side, then switch.

Common Mistakes

Front Foot Too Close
Step out until the bottom feels stable and your heel stays down.
Pushing off the Back Leg
Keep the work on the front leg.
Short Range
Go down until your back knee is just above the floor.

Questions

What muscles does the Bulgarian split squat work?

Mainly the quads and glutes of the front leg, with the adductors and hamstrings helping.

How do I work the glutes more?

Take a slightly longer stance and lean your torso forward a little. A shorter stance with an upright torso works the quads more.

Why is it so hard to balance?

Most people put their feet in one line. Give yourself a little width between the front foot and the back foot, like standing on train tracks.
