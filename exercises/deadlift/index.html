Barbell Deadlift

The deadlift picks a loaded bar up from the floor. It trains more muscle at once than almost any other lift and is the heaviest of the three powerlifts for most people.

How to Do It

1
Stand at the BarMid-foot under the bar, feet about hip width.
2
GripHinge down and grip just outside your legs.
3
Shins to the BarBend your knees until your shins touch it. Back flat, chest up.
4
Take the Slack OutPull your shoulders down and back to tighten your lats.
5
Push the Floor AwayKeep the bar against your legs as it rises.
6
Lock OutStand tall with hips and knees straight, then lower the same way.

Common Mistakes

Rounding the Lower Back
Lower the weight and keep your back flat from the first inch.
Bar Drifting Forward
Keep it against your legs the whole way.
Hips Shooting Up First
Raise your chest and hips together.
Leaning Back at the Top
Finish by standing straight, not by bending backward.

Questions

What muscles does the deadlift work?

The glutes, hamstrings and lower back do most of the work, with the quads, lats, upper back, traps and forearms helping.

Conventional or sumo?

Both are fine. Sumo uses a wide stance and a more upright torso; conventional has a narrower stance. Most people are stronger in one, so train the one that feels better.

Should I use straps?

Straps help on heavy sets when your grip gives out before your back and legs. Train some sets without them so your grip keeps up.
