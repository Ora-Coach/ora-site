Lat Pulldown

A cable pull from overhead. It builds the lats and upper back, and is a lighter way to train toward pull-ups.

How to Do It

1
Set the PadAdjust the thigh pad so your legs are locked in snugly.
2
GripA little wider than your shoulders, palms facing away.
3
Sit DownArms straight overhead, slight lean back.
4
PullBring the bar to your upper chest, elbows down and back.
5
SqueezeHold for a moment without leaning further back.
6
Let It RiseUnder control until your arms are straight.

Common Mistakes

Leaning Way Back
Keep a small lean and pull down, not back.
Pulling Behind the Neck
Pull to the front. Behind the neck strains the shoulders.
Cutting the Stretch
Let your arms straighten fully at the top.

Questions

What muscles does the lat pulldown work?

Mainly the lats, with the biceps, rear delts and upper back helping.

Wide or close grip?

Both work the lats. A close or neutral grip lets the arms help more and often allows more weight; a wide grip shortens the range a little.

Is the lat pulldown as good as pull-ups?

For building the lats, yes. It's easier to load lightly and add weight in small jumps, which makes it a good way to work toward pull-ups.
