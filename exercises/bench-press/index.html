Barbell Bench Press

The classic upper-body strength lift. Done well, it builds the chest, shoulders and triceps and lets you add weight for years.

How to Do It

1
Set UpLie with your eyes under the bar and your feet flat on the floor.
2
Lock Your ShouldersPull your shoulder blades back and down. Keep a slight arch in your upper back.
3
GripHands a little wider than your shoulders, wrists stacked over your elbows.
4
UnrackHold the bar over your shoulders with straight arms.
5
LowerBring it under control to your lower chest, elbows about 45-70° from your sides.
6
PressTouch without bouncing, then press up and slightly back.
REMEMBERShoulder Blades PinnedBar to Lower ChestFeet Drive Into the Floor

Common Mistakes

Elbows Flared Straight Out
Tuck them a little so your forearms stay vertical.
Bouncing off the Chest
Touch, then press. Bouncing hides weak reps.
Butt Lifting off the Bench
Drive through your feet, not your hips.
Shoulders Rolling Forward
Keep your shoulder blades pinned for the whole set.

Questions

What muscles does the bench press work?

Mainly the chest (pecs), with the front of the shoulders and the triceps helping. The upper back and lats keep the bar path steady.

Should the bar touch my chest?

Yes, for a full rep. Lower it to the lower chest, touch without bouncing, and press.

How wide should my grip be?

Most people do best a little wider than shoulder width, with forearms vertical at the bottom. Very wide grips shorten the range and stress the shoulders.
