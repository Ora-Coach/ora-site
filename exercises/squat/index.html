Barbell Back Squat

The back squat trains the whole lower body under a barbell. It is one of the best lifts for leg strength and size, and one of the three powerlifts.

How to Do It

1
Set the BarRack it at about mid-chest height and rest it on your upper back, not your neck.
2
Grip and TightenHands just outside your shoulders. Squeeze your upper back.
3
Walk OutTwo or three steps back. Feet about shoulder width, toes turned out a little.
4
BraceBig breath into your belly and tighten as if about to be punched.
5
Sit DownKnees track over your toes until your hip crease is at or below your knees.
6
Stand UpDrive through your whole foot with your chest up. Breathe out at the top.

Common Mistakes

Knees Caving In
Push your knees out over your toes on the way down and up.
Heels Lifting
Keep your weight over mid-foot. Try a wider stance or lifting shoes.
Cutting Depth
Keep every rep the same depth, even as the set gets hard.
Losing the Brace
Re-brace before every rep, not just the first.

Questions

What muscles do squats work?

Mostly the quads and glutes, with the adductors (inner thighs) helping and the lower back and core holding your torso upright.

How deep should I squat?

As deep as you can with a neutral lower back. For most people that is hip crease at or below the knee, which is also the powerlifting standard.

Is it bad if my knees go past my toes?

No. Knees moving forward is normal, especially for longer legs. What matters is that they track in line with your toes and your heels stay down.
