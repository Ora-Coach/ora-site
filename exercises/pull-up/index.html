Pull-Up

The pull-up lifts your whole body to a bar. It is one of the best bodyweight exercises for the back and arms.

How to Do It

1
HangHands a little wider than your shoulders, palms facing away.
2
Set Your ShouldersStart from straight arms with your shoulders pulled slightly down.
3
PullDrive your elbows toward your ribs to lift your chest to the bar.
4
Clear the BarChin over the bar without craning your neck.
5
LowerUnder control until your arms are straight again.
REMEMBERFull Hang to Chin OverElbows to RibsNo Swinging

Common Mistakes

Half Reps
Go from straight arms to chin over the bar every rep.
Kipping or Swinging
Keep your body still so your back does the work.
Dropping Fast
Take about two seconds on the way down.

Questions

What muscles do pull-ups work?

Mainly the lats, with the biceps, upper back, rear delts, forearms and core helping.

Pull-up or chin-up?

Pull-ups use an overhand grip; chin-ups use an underhand grip, which lets the biceps help so most people can do a few more.

How do I get my first pull-up?

Practice slow negatives (jump to the top, lower for 5 seconds), band-assisted reps and lat pulldowns two or three times a week. Add weight once you can do 12 clean reps.
