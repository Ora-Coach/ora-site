Pull-Up

The pull-up lifts your whole body to a bar. It is one of the best bodyweight exercises for the back and arms.

Main musclesLats
EquipmentPull-up bar
TypeVertical pull

Muscles worked

MainLats
HelpingBicepsUpper backRear deltsForearms (grip)Core

How to do it

  1. Hang from the bar with hands a little wider than shoulder width, palms facing away.
  2. Start from straight arms with your shoulders pulled slightly down.
  3. Pull your elbows down toward your ribs to lift your chest to the bar.
  4. Get your chin over the bar without craning your neck.
  5. Lower yourself under control until your arms are straight again.

Common mistakes

  • Half reps. Go from straight arms to chin over the bar each rep.
  • Kipping or swinging. Uses momentum instead of the back. Keep the body still.
  • Dropping fast. The lowering half builds strength too. Take about two seconds.

Sets and reps

  • Muscle: 3-4 sets of 5-12 reps, stopping 1-3 reps short of failure. Add weight once you can do 12.
  • Can't do one yet: slow lowering reps (5-second negatives), band-assisted pull-ups or lat pulldowns, 3 sets each, 2-3 times a week.

Questions

What muscles do pull-ups work?

Mainly the lats, with the biceps, upper back, rear delts, forearms and core helping.

Pull-up or chin-up?

Pull-ups use an overhand grip; chin-ups use an underhand grip, which lets the biceps help so most people can do a few more.

How do I get my first pull-up?

Practice slow negatives (jump to the top, lower for 5 seconds), band-assisted reps and lat pulldowns a few times a week.
