Barbell Overhead Press

The overhead press pushes a barbell from your shoulders to straight arms overhead, standing. It builds the shoulders and triceps and a strong, braced core.

How to Do It

1
Set UpBar at upper-chest height, grip just outside your shoulders.
2
UnrackBar on the front of your shoulders, elbows slightly in front of it.
3
BraceFeet hip width. Squeeze your glutes and tighten your core.
4
PressStraight up, moving your head back slightly to let the bar pass.
5
Lock OutHead forward under the bar, arms straight over mid-foot.
6
LowerUnder control back to your shoulders.
REMEMBERGlutes TightHead Through at the TopBar Over Mid-Foot

Common Mistakes

Leaning Way Back
Glutes tight and ribs down, so it stays a shoulder press.
Pressing Forward
The bar travels in a straight line over mid-foot.
Soft Lockout
Push all the way up with the bar over your shoulders.

Questions

What muscles does the overhead press work?

Mainly the front and side delts, with the triceps, upper chest and traps helping, and the core holding you upright.

Should I press standing or seated?

Standing trains the core and is the classic strength lift. Seated lets you focus on the shoulders and often use a little more weight.

Why is my overhead press so much weaker than my bench?

That's normal. The shoulders are smaller muscles than the chest, and many lifters press roughly 60-70% of what they bench.
