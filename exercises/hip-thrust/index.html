Barbell Hip Thrust

The hip thrust trains the glutes through a full hip extension with your upper back on a bench. It loads the glutes heavily without loading the spine.

How to Do It

1
Sit Against the BenchUpper back on the edge, just below your shoulder blades.
2
Roll the Bar InA padded barbell over the crease of your hips.
3
Set Your FeetHip width, close enough that your shins are vertical at the top.
4
Drive UpChin tucked, push through your heels to lift your hips.
5
Lock OutStraight line from shoulders to knees. Squeeze your glutes.
6
LowerUnder control until your hips are just above the floor.
REMEMBERChin TuckedShins Vertical at the TopSqueeze and Pause

Common Mistakes

Arching at the Top
Finish with your glutes, ribs down, not by bending your spine.
Feet Too Far Out
Keep your shins vertical at the top so the glutes do the work.
Rushing the Top
Pause for a second at the top of each rep.

Questions

What muscles does the hip thrust work?

Mainly the glutes, with the hamstrings, adductors and quads helping.

Hip thrust or glute bridge?

The hip thrust uses a bench for a bigger range of motion and usually more load. The glute bridge is easier to set up and a good place to start.

The bar hurts my hips. What helps?

Use a thick bar pad or a folded mat, and set the bar in the crease of your hips rather than on the hip bones.
