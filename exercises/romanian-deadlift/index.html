Romanian Deadlift

The Romanian deadlift starts standing and lowers the bar by pushing the hips back. It is one of the best lifts for the hamstrings and glutes, and it teaches a strong hip hinge.

How to Do It

1
Stand TallHold the bar at your thighs, hands just outside your legs, feet hip width.
2
Soften Your KneesUnlock them slightly and keep that bend for the whole rep.
3
Push Your Hips BackLet your torso tip forward with your back flat.
4
Slide the Bar DownKeep it touching, or nearly touching, your legs.
5
Feel the StretchStop at a strong hamstring stretch, usually near the knees or mid-shin.
6
Hips ForwardDrive them forward to stand up and squeeze your glutes.
REMEMBERHips Back, Not DownBar Close to LegsSame Knee Bend

Common Mistakes

Squatting It Down
Keep the same slight knee bend and move at the hips.
Rounding to Reach Lower
Stop where your back would start to round.
Bar Drifting Forward
Keep it close so the hamstrings take the load.
Leaning Back at the Top
Stand straight and squeeze your glutes.

Questions

What muscles does the Romanian deadlift work?

Mainly the hamstrings and glutes, with the lower back holding your spine flat and the grip and lats keeping the bar close.

What is the difference between a Romanian deadlift and a deadlift?

A deadlift starts each rep from the floor with more knee bend. A Romanian deadlift starts standing, keeps the knees nearly straight and lowers only as far as your hamstrings allow, so it works the hamstrings more.

How low should I go on an RDL?

Until you feel a strong stretch in your hamstrings with a flat back. For most people that is around the knees to mid-shin.
