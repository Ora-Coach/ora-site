Romanian Deadlift

The Romanian deadlift starts standing and lowers the bar by pushing the hips back. It is one of the best lifts for the hamstrings and glutes, and it teaches a strong hip hinge.

Main musclesHamstrings, Glutes
EquipmentBarbell or dumbbells
TypeHip hinge

Muscles worked

MainHamstringsGlutes
HelpingLower backForearms (grip)Lats

How to do it

  1. Stand tall holding the bar at your thighs, hands just outside your legs, feet hip width.
  2. Unlock your knees slightly and keep that bend for the whole rep.
  3. Push your hips straight back and let your torso tip forward, back flat.
  4. Slide the bar down your thighs, keeping it touching or nearly touching your legs.
  5. Stop when you feel a strong stretch in your hamstrings, usually around mid-shin or just below the knee.
  6. Drive your hips forward to stand back up, squeezing your glutes at the top.

Common mistakes

  • Squatting it down. Bending the knees more as you lower turns it into a deadlift. Keep the same slight bend and move at the hips.
  • Rounding the back to reach lower. Range comes from the hips, not the spine. Stop where your back would start to round.
  • Bar drifting forward. Keep it close to your legs so the hamstrings, not the lower back, take the load.
  • Leaning back at the top. Stand straight and squeeze the glutes; there is nothing to gain past vertical.

Sets and reps

  • Muscle: 3-4 sets of 6-12 reps, stopping 1-3 reps short of failure.
  • Strength: 3-4 sets of 5-8 reps.
  • Beginners: 3 sets of 8-10 reps with a light bar or dumbbells, learning the hinge first.

Questions

What muscles does the Romanian deadlift work?

Mainly the hamstrings and glutes, with the lower back holding your spine flat and the grip and lats keeping the bar close.

What is the difference between a Romanian deadlift and a deadlift?

A deadlift starts each rep from the floor with more knee bend. A Romanian deadlift starts standing, keeps the knees nearly straight and lowers only as far as your hamstrings allow, so it works the hamstrings more.

How low should I go on an RDL?

Until you feel a strong stretch in your hamstrings with a flat back. For most people that is around the knees to mid-shin. Going lower by rounding the back does not help.
