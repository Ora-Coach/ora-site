How to load a barbell

  • Take off the bar, then halve it. Subtract the bar's weight from the total and split the rest between the two sides.
  • Biggest plates go on first. Heavy plates sit closest to the collar and smaller ones go outside them. It keeps the bar steady and makes the load easy to read.
  • Load both sides the same. Count each side out loud. An extra 10 on one side is easy to miss and hard to balance.
  • Use clips. Plates slide on the sleeves, especially on presses and when a bar tilts. Clips stop that.

Pound and kilogram plates

1 kg is 2.2046 lb. Kilogram plates are color-coded in competition: 25 kg red, 20 kg blue, 15 kg yellow, 10 kg green and 5 kg white. Pound plates don't have a standard color, so read the number on the plate.

Questions

How do I work out what plates to put on the bar?

Take the bar's weight away from the total, then halve what's left. That's the weight for each side. Load it with the biggest plates first. For 225 lb on a 45 lb bar: 225 − 45 = 180, so 90 lb a side, which is two 45s.

How much does a barbell weigh?

A standard men's Olympic bar weighs 20 kg (44 lb), which US gyms count as 45 lb. A women's Olympic bar weighs 15 kg (33 lb), often counted as 35 lb. Technique, EZ curl, trap and Smith machine bars vary, so check the label.

Is a 45 lb plate the same as a 20 kg plate?

Close, but not the same. A 20 kg plate is about 44.1 lb and a 45 lb plate is about 20.4 kg. Over several plates the difference adds up, so don't mix them on one bar.

Why can't I load the exact weight I want?

Plates go on in pairs, so the smallest jump is twice your smallest plate: 5 lb with 2.5 lb plates, or 2.5 kg with 1.25 kg plates. The calculator shows the closest weights you can load with the plates you have.

How much do barbell collars weigh?

Spring clips weigh almost nothing. Competition collars used in powerlifting weigh 2.5 kg each, 5 kg for the pair, and count toward the total.
