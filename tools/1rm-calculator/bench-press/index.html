Getting a good bench estimate

  • Count clean reps. The bar touches your chest and you lock out, with your hips on the bench. Reps you bounced off your chest or only half locked out make the estimate read high.
  • Use a hard set of 2-6 reps. That range gives the closest estimate. A set of 15 tells you more about endurance than about your max.
  • Paused and touch-and-go are different lifts. Most people bench a little less with a pause on the chest. Powerlifting meets require the pause, so if you compete, estimate from paused sets.
  • Grip width changes the number. Close-grip bench usually moves less weight than a regular grip. Estimate each one on its own.

Warm up for a max attempt

Benching a max? Ramp up like this, with a spotter or the rack’s safety arms set just under your chest. Rest 2-3 minutes before the heavier sets and 3-5 before the attempt.

How the formulas compare

Different formulas give slightly different answers. For sets under about 10 reps they stay within a few percent of each other.

How the calculator works

The main estimate uses the Epley formula with reps in reserve, the same math Ora uses in the app:

1RM = weight × (1 + (reps + RIR) ÷ 30)

RIR (reps in reserve) is how many more reps you could have done. If you pick an RPE, RIR is 10 − RPE, so a set at RPE 8 counts as about 2 reps left. If you pick "Not sure", the calculator assumes the set was all-out, which gives a conservative estimate. A true single at RPE 10 is your max as lifted.

Questions

How do I find my bench press max without testing it?

Bench a set you can do for 2-6 good reps, note how many you had left (RPE), and enter it above. The estimate is close enough to plan training, and you skip the risk of failing a true max.

Do bounced reps count?

They inflate the estimate. Use reps where the bar touches your chest without bouncing off it, and where you fully lock out.

Are paused bench reps weaker than touch-and-go?

Usually a little, because the pause takes away the stretch at the bottom. If you compete in powerlifting, where the pause is required, estimate from paused sets.

Does this work for dumbbell bench press?

Yes for the estimate: enter the weight of one dumbbell and you get a one-dumbbell max. Ignore the plate loading, which is for a barbell.

What is RPE and RIR?

RPE rates how hard a set was on a 1-10 scale. RIR (reps in reserve) is how many more reps you could have done. RPE 8 means about 2 reps in reserve.

How accurate is a 1RM calculator?

It's an estimate. 1RM formulas are most accurate for sets of about 10 reps or fewer taken close to failure. Your true max on a given day can be a few percent higher or lower.
