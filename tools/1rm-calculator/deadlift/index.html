How to calculate your deadlift 1RM

You need one hard set: the weight, the reps, and how many more reps you could have done.

  1. After warming up, do a hard set of 2-6 good reps on the deadlift.
  2. Note how many reps you had left in the tank (RIR). RPE 8 means about 2 left.
  3. Add the reps you did to the reps you had left, divide by 30 and add 1, then multiply by the weight.

Example: 275 lb for 5 reps at RPE 8 (2 left): 275 × (1 + (5 + 2) ÷ 30) = 275 × 1.233 ≈ 339 lb. If those 5 reps were all-out, it’s 275 × (1 + 5 ÷ 30) ≈ 321 lb. In kilograms, 120 kg for 5 reps at RPE 8 comes to about 148 kg.

Getting a good deadlift estimate

  • Count reps you locked out. Standing tall with hips and knees straight. Reps that hitched up the thighs or never finished make the estimate read high.
  • Use a hard set of 2-5 reps. Grip and lower-back fatigue build fast on deadlifts, so long sets are an even rougher guide than on other lifts.
  • Conventional, sumo and trap bar are different lifts. Most people are stronger in one stance. Estimate each one on its own. Trap bars weigh different amounts, so add the bar's own weight to what you enter.
  • Straps change what's being measured. A set with straps estimates your strapped deadlift. If your grip gives out first, your max with no straps is lower.

Warm up for a max attempt

Pulling a max? Ramp up like this. The warm-up starts with full-size plates so the bar sits at its normal height off the floor. Rest 2-3 minutes before the heavier sets and 3-5 before the attempt.

How the formulas compare

Different formulas give slightly different answers. For sets under about 10 reps they stay within a few percent of each other.

How the calculator works

The main estimate uses the Epley formula with reps in reserve, the same math Ora uses in the app:

1RM = weight × (1 + (reps + RIR) ÷ 30)

RIR (reps in reserve) is how many more reps you could have done. If you pick an RPE, RIR is 10 − RPE, so a set at RPE 8 counts as about 2 reps left. If you pick "Not sure", the calculator assumes the set was all-out, which gives a conservative estimate. A true single at RPE 10 is your max as lifted.

Questions

How do I calculate my deadlift 1 rep max?

Take a hard set, add the reps you did to the reps you had left, divide by 30 and add 1, then multiply by the weight. For example, 275 lb for 5 reps with 2 left is 275 × (1 + 7 ÷ 30) ≈ 339 lb. The calculator above does this for you.

How do I find my deadlift max without testing it?

Pull a set you can do for 2-5 clean reps, note how many you had left (RPE), and enter it above. The estimate is close enough to plan training without the strain of a true max.

Is my sumo deadlift max the same as conventional?

Usually not. Most lifters are stronger in one stance, so estimate each from its own sets.

Do touch-and-go deadlift reps count?

Yes, as long as you don’t bounce the plates off the floor. Resetting each rep is a little harder, so reset reps give a slightly more conservative estimate.

How much does a trap bar weigh?

It depends on the model, so check the label or the maker’s site. Add the bar’s weight to the plates when you enter the weight lifted.

What is RPE and RIR?

RPE rates how hard a set was on a 1-10 scale. RIR (reps in reserve) is how many more reps you could have done. RPE 8 means about 2 reps in reserve.

How accurate is a 1RM calculator?

It's an estimate. 1RM formulas are most accurate for sets of about 10 reps or fewer taken close to failure. Your true max on a given day can be a few percent higher or lower.
